Data notes

Source. moengage-overview-2026-08-10_to_2026-11-23.xlsx (run 24 Nov 2026). Stats pulled in four windows: 10 Aug–8 Sep, 9 Sep–8 Oct, 9 Oct–7 Nov, 8 Nov–23 Nov. 78 event-triggered, periodic and one-time campaigns; one-time campaigns sent before 10 Aug are excluded.

WhatsApp. Sent = Sent, Delivered = Delivered, Read = Read. Read rate = read ÷ delivered. Click tracking returns 0 and no WhatsApp campaign has a conversion goal.

Email. Engagement = unique opens; clicks = unique clicks (summed across windows, so may slightly over-count). Sends with no delivery events are excluded from delivery rate and use sent as the open-rate base.

Conversions. MoEngage goal Shopify – Item Purchased, view-through attribution, email campaigns only. Five email campaigns carry all 19 conversions.

Flows. Flow analytics return trips started only (10 Aug – 7 Nov); completions and per-step channel sends are not available.

Not covered. On-site popups (no stats endpoint). No SMS, in-app or on-site campaign had activity in the window; the two Web Push campaigns had no sends.

Data notes

Source. pii-capture-trend-2026-11-23.xlsx, pulled from MoEngage user profiles. Dates are first-seen dates in Asia/Kolkata, 10 Aug – 23 Nov 2026.

Mobile captured. Profiles that currently hold a Mobile Number (Standard). This is today's status, not status at sign-up, so recent days can read slightly low.

Segment coverage. segment_pii_coverage_report.csv: MoEngage “PII-Report” segments by month (10–31 Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov to date). Coverage = users with that field ÷ users in the segment.

Bulk imports. 9 Oct (2,169 mobiles vs ~50 on a normal day) and 19 Nov (3,488 of 3,490 new users with mobile) look like list uploads, not on-site capture. They are shown separately as “organic”.
